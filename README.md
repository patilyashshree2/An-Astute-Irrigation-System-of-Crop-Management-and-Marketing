"An Astute Irrigation System With Crop Management And Marketing," is designed to assist farmers in improving agricultural productivity and profitability using smart technologies.

Key Functions of the Project:

Automatic Irrigation System:
- Automatically monitors and controls irrigation based on real-time soil moisture and temperature data.
- Operates a water pump via sensors to ensure optimal irrigation without manual intervention.

Crop Management:
- Provides farmers with recommendations on which crops to grow based on real-time soil conditions, previous market demands, and potential yield.
- Utilizes IoT sensors to capture necessary soil data.

Crop Marketing:
- Suggests the most profitable options for farmers to sell their produce, either at nearby markets or to industrial buyers.
- Calculates and displays total selling expenses and expected profits to help farmers maximize returns.

Technical Overview:
- The hardware comprises NodeMCU (ESP8266 Wi-Fi microcontroller), soil moisture and temperature sensors, ultrasonic water-level sensors, and a DC water pump.
- Data from sensors are transmitted wirelessly, processed locally, and then stored in a cloud database.
- A cross-platform mobile application developed using Xamarin provides users with real-time data visualizations and actionable insights, accessible in multiple languages (English, Hindi, Marathi).

Goal and Benefits:

- Automates traditional farming methods, enhancing productivity and sustainability.
- Aims to boost farmers' profits by integrating crop selection and market analytics.
- Uses cost-effective IoT technology, making it affordable and scalable for widespread adoption by farmers.
